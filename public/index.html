How it works

Sign in

Create a free account. Your preferences and printer links are tied to it.

Run the agent on your PC

A tiny program that exposes your printer and model folder to the app — over a secure tunnel, tied to your account.

The app links to your PC

Once paired, your account's app talks to your computer. Every user connects to their own machine, respectively.

Find → slice → print

Bring in a model, slice it for the 5M, and send it — with a full view of what's happening.
